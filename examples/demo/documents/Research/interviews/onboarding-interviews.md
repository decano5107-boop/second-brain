# Onboarding interviews — synthesis

Six participants, all of whom hike at least once a month; four had tried a competing app.

## What we heard

- Nobody wants to create an account before seeing a trail. Five of six said so unprompted.
- Offline maps matter more than social features. "No signal" was the most common worry.
- Permission prompts for location felt pushy when they came before any value was shown.

## What it means for the redesign

Show a nearby trail first, ask for location when the user taps "start", and move account
creation to the moment they want to save a hike.
