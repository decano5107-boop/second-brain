# Harbor Bakery — rebrand proposal

Prepared for the owners of Harbor Bakery, a two-location neighbourhood bakery.

## Scope

1. Brand audit: current logo, signage, packaging and the online menu.
2. Three identity directions, one refined to final: wordmark, colour palette, type pairing.
3. Packaging system for boxes, bags and the loyalty card.
4. A one-page brand guide the staff can actually use.

## Timeline

Six weeks from kickoff. Direction review at the end of week two; packaging proofs in week five.

## Fee

Fixed fee in three instalments: 40% at kickoff, 30% at direction sign-off, 30% on delivery.
Printing is billed by the printer directly.
